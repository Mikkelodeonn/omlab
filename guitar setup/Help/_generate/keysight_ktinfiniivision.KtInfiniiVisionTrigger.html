Automatically sets the trigger levels of all displayed analog channels to their waveforms 50% values.

Returns:

None

Remarks:

• When High and Low (dual) trigger levels are used (as with Rise/Fall Time and Runt triggers, for example), this method has no effect.
• If AC coupling is used, the trigger levels are set to 0 V.

property continuous¶

If true, the oscilloscope waits trigger holdoff seconds after a waveform acquisition is complete and then immediately enters the wait for trigger state without passing through the idle state.

Access:

Type:

property coupling¶

Sets and gets the current oscilloscope coupling selection.

Access:

ReadWrite

Type:

TriggerCoupling

Remarks:

• AC coupling places a high-pass filter (10 Hz for analog channels, and 3.5 Hz for all External trigger inputs) in the trigger path, removing dc offset voltage from the trigger waveform. Use AC coupling to get a stable edge trigger when your waveform has a large dc offset.
• LFReject coupling places a 50 KHz high-pass filter in the trigger path.
• DC coupling allows dc and ac signals into the trigger path.
• This property and TriggerEdgeRejectFilterType can change the coupling setting.

property hf_reject_filter_enabled¶

Enables or disables the high frequency reject filter whicj adds a 50 kHz low-pass filter in the trigger path to remove high frequency components from the trigger waveform.

Access:

ReadWrite

Type:

bool

Remarks:

• Use this filter to remove high-frequency noise, such as AM or FM broadcast stations, from the trigger path.
• This property is not valid in TV trigger mode.
property holdoff¶

Sets and gets the holdoff time (from 40 ns to 10 s) value for the seleted trigger mode. The units are seconds.

Access:

ReadWrite

Type:

float

Remarks:

• It is the length of time to wait after detecting a trigger before enabling the trigger subsystem again.
• Holdoff keeps a trigger from occurring until after a certain amount of time has passed since the last trigger.
• This feature is valuable when a waveform crosses the trigger level multiple times during one period of the waveform.
• Without holdoff, the oscilloscope could trigger on each of the crossings, producing a confusing waveform.
• With holdoff set correctly, the oscilloscope always triggers on the same crossing. The correct holdoff setting is typically slightly less than one period.
property jitter_free_enabled¶

Enables or disables the Jitter free trigger.

Access:

ReadWrite

Type:

bool

Remarks:
  • Oscilloscope trigger circuitry introduces a certain amount of noise and jitter that result in horizontal error. To correct for this error, in certain trigger modes and at certain timebase ranges, hardware Jitter-Free Trigger correction is used to minimize trigger jitter at the trigger point.
  • The effect seen on the oscilloscope display is that all plotted acquisitions cross through the same pixel location at the intersection of the trigger level and time=0.
  • Jitter: Free Trigger correction works well for fast digital edges, but user may want to turn it off for some slow slew rate analog signals.
    • property level¶

    Sets and gets the Voltage threshold for the trigger subsystem. The units are volts.

    Access:

    ReadWrite

    Type:

    float

    Remarks:

    • For Internal Triggers: <level> wot be 0.75 x full-scale voltage from center screen.
    • For External Triggers: ±(external range setting)
    • For MSO Models: ±8 V for digital channels.
    • If the optional source is specified and is not the active source, the level on the active source is not affected and the active source is not changed.
    property modifier¶

    Sets and gets the Trigger sweep mode. The trigger modifier determines the oscilloscope’s behavior in the absence of a trigger.

    Access:

    ReadWrite

    Type:

    TriggerModifier

    Remarks:

    • When Auto sweep mode is selected, a baseline is displayed in the absence of a signal. If a signal is present but the oscilloscope is not triggered, the unsynchronized signal is displayed instead of a baseline.
    • When Normal sweep mode is selected and no trigger is present, the instrument mdoes not sweep, and the data acquired on the previous trigger remains on the screen.
    • This feature is similar to "Mode" on the instrument's front panel.
