class KtInfiniiVisionJitterRealTimeEye¶

KtInfiniiVisionJitterRealTimeEye interface.

acquisition(self: keysight_ktinfiniivision.keysight_ktinfiniivision.KtInfiniiVisionJitterRealTimeEye) → None¶

Starts building the real-time eye.

Returns:

None

Remarks:
When building the real-time eye, full-length acquisitions are used to get the largest acquisition record possible, resulting in the most accurate clock recovery and eye measurements possible.
