KtInfiniiVisionWaveGenModulationAM¶

Access interface members using: driver.wave_gens[<name_or_index>].modulation.am
Where driver is a reference to the driver’s root interface returned by the constructor.
class KtInfiniiVisionWaveGenModulationAM¶

KtInfiniiVisionWaveGenModulationAM interface.

property depth¶

Sets and gets the amount of amplitude modulation.

Access:

ReadWrite

Type:

float

Remarks:

• AM Depth refers to the portion of the amplitude range that will be used by the modulation. For example, a depth setting of 80% causes the output amplitude to vary from 10% to 90% (90% ? 10% = 80%) of the original amplitude as the modulating signal goes from its minimum to maximum amplitude.
• Input ranges from 0 to 100.
• On T3000, X4000, X6000, M9240X and P9240X series oscilloscopes it supports both waveform generators (i.e. WaveGen1, WaveGen2).
• On X1000, X1200, X2000 and X3000 series oscilloscopes it supports one waveform generator only (i.e WaveGen1).
property frequency¶

Sets and gets the frequency of the modulating signal. Frequency in Hz .

Access:

ReadWrite

Type:

float

Remarks:

• On T3000, X4000, X6000, M9240X and P9240X series oscilloscopes it supports both waveform generators (i.e. WaveGen1, WaveGen2).
• On X1000, X1200, X2000 and X3000 series oscilloscopes it supports one waveform generator only (i.e WaveGen1).
